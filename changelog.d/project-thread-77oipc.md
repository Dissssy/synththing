### Added
- Clean up: "On a matched album" rows. Once one song of an album is matched at MusicBrainz, songs on no album whose titles are its missing tracks (and whose track numbers, lengths or import line up too) are offered to match to it, a row for each album, so their album, artist and numbers are filled in at once.
- Album pages: right-click a greyed-out track that isn't in your library and pick "Match a song in your library..." to pick the song that is it, the likeliest first.
- Online lookup: songs with a title and track number but no album or artist are each looked up once by their title. When three or more of them line up with one release's tracks, title and number alike, they're matched to it and get their album, artist and details.

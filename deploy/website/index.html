YOUR MUSIC

One library for everything you own

Drop it in once and it just works, forever.

Plays every format

MP3, FLAC, WAV, OGG, Opus, M4A/AAC/ALAC, AIFF, WMA and MIDI, side by side in one list. Songs start almost instantly.

Drag, drop, done

Drop files or whole folders (subfolders too) onto the window. synththing keeps its own safe copy, so moving or renaming your originals never breaks a playlist.

Duplicates handled

Every song is recognised by its contents, so the same track imported twice is still one song.

Columns your way

Artist, album, length, year, genre, bit rate and more. Click to sort, right-click to choose columns or group by album, genre or tag.

Stars and tags

Star your favourites with one click, label songs with your own coloured tags, and set shuffle to play only starred songs.

Album art everywhere

Covers come from your files or online. Anything without art gets a tidy coloured tile of its own.

SEARCH

Find any song in a second

Type a few letters, or ask very specific questions.

Forgiving search

Typos are fine: "daft pk" still finds Daft Punk, and matches light up in the list.

Power filters

Ask for exactly what you mean: year:90s, length:>6:00, added:30d, is:starred, -type:mid.

It finishes your sentence

The search box suggests filters, then your actual artists, albums, genres and tags as you type.

Search becomes a playlist

One click turns any search into a smart playlist that keeps itself up to date.

PLAYLISTS

Playlists that build themselves

Make the rules once; synththing keeps them filled.

Smart playlists

"Everything from the 2000s I added this month that isn't MIDI." New songs join automatically.

A playlist for every artist

Automatic pages for every artist, album, genre, year, composer or tag, with album covers, all browsable in Groupings.

Up next queue

Play next or add to queue from any song, album, artist or playlist, then drag to reorder. Your playlist carries on afterwards.

Picks up where you left off

Each playlist remembers the song it was on, and long songs can remember their position.

Keep tracks together

Link an intro to its song, or a medley's parts, and shuffle always plays them as one.

Per-song fine tuning

Trim a song's start and end, nudge its volume, or keep it out of shuffle.

AUTO TIDY

A messy library that cleans itself

Years of badly named downloads, sorted out quietly in the background.

Automatic matching

synththing slowly works through your whole library, matches songs against MusicBrainz's huge music database, and fills in missing albums, years and genres.

Real album covers

Pulls official covers from the Cover Art Archive, with iTunes art to choose from if you prefer another.

Clean-up suggestions

The broom spots problems: "Artist & Guest" split into two artists, names spelled two ways, track numbers stuck in titles, "(Official Video)" leftovers.

Reads file names

Songs with no tags at all, like 34. Title - Artist, get their details worked out from the name.

Edit many at once

Select a whole album and fix its artist, year or genre in one go. Nothing is ever changed without your say-so.

Sorted like a record shop

The Beatles file under B, compilations go under Various Artists, and classical can be filed by composer.

EVERY DEVICE

Your music follows you, privately

Windows, plus Linux and Android builds that are still marked experimental.

Sign in with a QR code

No passwords. Scan a code from your PC with your phone and it's you on both.

Songs travel by themselves

Add a song on one device and it shows up on the others: your playlists, your starred songs, or your whole library.

End-to-end encrypted

Everything is locked on your device before it leaves, so the server can't even tell which songs you have.

Everything stays in step

Playlists, settings, themes, keyboard shortcuts and song details sync, with per-device choices for anything you want to keep separate.

A real phone app

Plays in the background with lock screen and Bluetooth controls, and floats the visualizer in picture-in-picture when you switch apps.

Remove everywhere

Delete a song from one device or from all of them at once. Your choice every time.

VISUALIZERS

A light show you can play

Every song comes with something to watch, and some you can play along with.

Built-in visualizers

Waveform, spectrum, spectrogram, piano keyboard, disco, pulse, letters, a full dashboard, windowed or fullscreen.

Games that use your music

A Guitar Hero style highway charted from any song, a platformer that runs to the notes, snake, a piano you play with your keyboard, and yes, Doom (bring your own WAD). Game controllers work too.

Record it as a video

Turn the visualizer and its sound into an MP4, from 720p to 4K, 30 or 60 fps, vertical for phones, for one song or a whole playlist.

Make your own

Visualizers are small Lua scripts with a live-reloading editor and a built-in pixel-art sprite editor. No setup.

Share and download

Browse scripts other people made, install them in one click, publish yours, and get their updates automatically.

STREAMING

Ready for your stream

Show what you're listening to without screen capture tricks.

Straight into OBS

Add the visualizer as a Browser Source. It's drawn at exactly the size your scene needs.

See-through overlays

Visualizers can have transparent backgrounds, so they sit right on top of your game or camera.

Now playing card

A pixel-art card with the title, artist, time and album cover, coloured to match the album.

Every language

Japanese, Korean and Chinese song names show properly, in the same style as everything else.

MIDI

MIDI that sounds the way you want

Where synththing started, and still the best place to play a .mid.

Any soundfont

Play MIDI through any SoundFont you like and switch with a click. A song can keep a soundfont of its own.

Starter pack

The first launch offers two soundfonts and four songs, so there's music playing within a minute.

Notes you can see

Visualizers and games read the actual notes of a MIDI song, not just its sound.

CONTROL

Control it from anywhere

Your keyboard, your media keys, your phone, your smart home.

Media keys and system controls

Works with keyboard media keys, Windows' volume overlay and Linux desktop widgets.

Never touch the mouse

Every action has a shortcut, and a command palette (Ctrl+Shift+P) finds anything by name.

Your keys, everywhere

Rebind any shortcut, chain keys like Ctrl+G then S, and your layout syncs to your other PCs.

Plugs into your setup

A built-in local API lets home automation, Stream Deck style tools or your own scripts see and control what's playing, with permissions you tick per program.

MAKE IT YOURS

Looks the way you like

Arrange it, theme it, and forget about upkeep.

Themes

Dark, Light and a glossy Frutiger Aero, or build your own down to every colour, corner and shadow.

Move anything

Every panel is a tab you can drag, split or pop out, with ready-made layouts for listening, watching or creating.

Updates itself

One file, no installer. New versions are offered inside the app, and a guided tour shows you around on day one.

Free, for real

No ads, no subscription, open source. Feedback goes straight from the app to the people building it.

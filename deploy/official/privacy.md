# Privacy Policy

*synththing official library (synththing.p51.nl). Last updated 2026-10-07.*

The synththing library is built to need as little about you as possible: there are no accounts, no passwords, no ads and no tracking. This page says what the server does keep, why, for how long, and how to have it deleted.

## Who runs it

The official library server is run by the makers of synththing. Questions, requests and complaints: **contact@p51.nl**.

## What the server keeps

**When you only browse** (search, read, install), the server keeps nothing about you, apart from counting how often each script is installed and each song downloaded (fetching a song to play its preview isn't counted). The app talks to the server while the Online Library is open, when you publish, when you check a script for updates, and, while its Notifications preference is on and you have an identity, to fetch your notifications when it starts and to keep a connection open for new ones while it runs.

**Notifications** the server keeps for you (encores, installs and downloads adding up, your scripts and songs hidden or back, remixes of your scripts, your reports dealt with) are deleted 90 days after you've read them.

**Your identity** is a key your app makes; the server sees its public half (shown as an ID like `#k3f9q2xa`) on anything you sign. It's not linked to your name, email or device.

**When you publish**, the server keeps the script or song (a song's MIDI file, as it is: any text in it, like a copyright notice, included), its versions, its name, description, tags and category, for a song its credits and the rights you declared, the name you posted under, and your identity's ID. These are public: that's what publishing is.

**Encores and reports**: which identity gave which script or song an encore (only the totals are shown publicly), and the reports you send (their reason, details and what they point at), which only the server's moderators see.

**Your address (IP)** is kept with each upload, encore, report and email request, to limit abuse (how many of each one address can make) and so moderators can ban someone who abuses the library. Addresses are deleted after **30 days**, except those of active bans, kept until the ban ends. The server's own log also notes uploads, with the address they came from; the system rotates it away.

**Your email, if you attach one** for recovering your identity: the server keeps only a one-way hash of it (salted with a secret of the server's), never the address itself, so it can recognise the address when you type it again but can't read or show it, and can't write to you unasked. When you ask for a code (to attach an address, change it, recover your identity or delete your data), the address you type is handed to our email provider to send it, and not kept.

**Identity changes**: when your identity moves to a new key (New key..., or a recovery), the record of which key became which is kept, and shown to other servers so your scripts follow you.

## Who else sees it

- **Everyone**: what you publish, the names you post under, your ID, and encore totals.
- **The server's moderators**: reports, and for each script and song the addresses its versions were uploaded from (while they're kept).
- **Service providers**, only as far as running the library needs: the server is hosted by Hetzner Online (in the United States); emails are sent through SendGrid (Twilio). Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared for advertising.

## Deleting your data

In the app, Preferences > Library > **Delete my data...** has the server delete what it has of yours: your scripts and songs, the encores you gave, your admin rights and your email hash. It's hidden at once and deleted for good after **30 days**; until then, **Restore my data...** puts it all back. If your identity has an email attached, a code sent to it confirms the deletion. Reports you made are kept, without your identity on them, and bans are kept until they end.

Lost your key? Recover your identity by email first (Preferences > Library > Recover...), then delete. If you can't, write to contact@p51.nl.

You can also ask us what the server has about you, or to correct it, at contact@p51.nl.
